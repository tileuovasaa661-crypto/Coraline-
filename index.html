The Three Ghost Children

Three trapped souls from other worlds who beg Coraline to find their stolen eyes and set them free.

A House With Many Doors

When Coraline Jones moves into the Pink Palace Apartments, she finds a small door bricked over in the wall. One night, it opens onto a passage leading to another apartment just like her own — but better. The food tastes richer. Her parents are kinder. Everyone has buttons sewn over their eyes.

At first the Other World feels like a dream come true. But the longer Coraline stays, the more she realizes that everything she loves there comes with a price, and the Other Mother has no intention of letting her go home.
